# Prompturi pentru logo, DorDeFranceza

Trei prompturi gata de copiat, pentru orice generator de imagini (gpt-image,
Midjourney, Ideogram, Seedream). Sunt scrise în engleză pentru că toate
modelele lucrează cel mai bine așa.

**De reținut înainte de a le folosi.** Un generator de imagini scoate idei, nu
logouri finale. Literele ies deseori strâmbe sau scrise greșit, iar fișierul
e o poză, nu un vector. Alegi ideea care îți place din planșă, iar logoul
final se desenează curat în SVG, ca să meargă la orice mărime, de la favicon
la o pancartă.

Paleta e cea a site-ului: crem `#f8f3eb`, indigo `#4865ff`, cerneală
`#080331`, corai `#ff6d39`, roz `#f098d7`, verde `#2c7a34`.

---

## 1. Planșă cu nouă simboluri

Pentru găsit direcția. Fără litere, acolo unde generatoarele se descurcă cel
mai bine.

```
Logo exploration sheet for a premium online French-tutoring brand for Romanian
adults. Warm, editorial, humanist, not corporate and not cute.

LAYOUT: flat 2D presentation board on a warm cream background (#f8f3eb). A
3 x 3 grid of nine separate logo marks, each isolated and centred in its own
generous cell of empty space. No frames, no boxes, no borders, no captions, no
numbers, no text of any kind anywhere on the board.

THE NINE MARKS:
1. a lowercase letter o wearing a French acute accent above it
2. two French guillemet quotation marks facing each other to enclose a circle
3. a single French acute accent, enlarged, drawn as one confident tapered stroke
4. a swallow in flight drawn as one continuous unbroken line
5. an interlocking monogram of the capital letters D and F
6. a slim Parisian arched doorway whose left side also reads as the letter D
7. a circle divided into two facing crescents, like two people in conversation
8. a speech bubble built from two guillemet marks
9. a seed with one sprouting leaf, geometric and simple

COLOUR: each mark in ONE flat colour, either vivid indigo blue (#4865ff) or
near-black violet (#080331). Two or three of the marks may carry a single small
accent shape in warm coral (#ff6d39) or soft pink (#f098d7). Nothing else.

STYLE: contemporary editorial branding, Pentagram-grade minimalism,
Scandinavian restraint, geometric precision, even confident stroke weights,
generous negative space, perfectly flat. Each mark must still read clearly at
16 pixels.

DO NOT INCLUDE: no gradients, no 3D, no bevels, no drop shadows, no glow, no
texture, no photography, no mockups, no hands, no Eiffel Tower, no croissants,
no berets, no flags, no tricolour stripes, no lettering, no brand name, no
tagline, no watermark, no signature.
```

## 2. Planșă cu numele scris

Pentru văzut cum arată numele în serif editorial. Aici verifică litera cu
litera: modelele scriu des greșit.

```
Typography logo lockup sheet on a warm cream background (#f8f3eb). Four
wordmark variations stacked vertically with generous spacing, each centred
horizontally, nothing else on the board.

THE WORDMARK: the single word DorDeFranceza, spelled exactly
D-o-r-D-e-F-r-a-n-c-e-z-a as one word, set in a refined editorial serif at
regular weight, elegant and high-contrast, in the spirit of Source Serif or
Freight Display. The middle syllable De is set in italic while Dor and
Franceza stay upright. Colour near-black violet (#080331). The letter o in Dor
carries a small French acute accent in vivid indigo (#4865ff), which is the
brand's signature detail.

THE FOUR VARIATIONS, top to bottom:
1. the wordmark alone
2. a small indigo circular mark placed to the left of the wordmark
3. the same small mark centred above the wordmark
4. the wordmark with a short single line of small grey sans-serif text beneath

STYLE: flat vector typography specimen, precise letterforms, correct spelling,
generous letter-spacing, no distortion, editorial, calm, expensive.

DO NOT INCLUDE: no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no texture, no mockups, no
extra words, no Eiffel Tower, no flags, no watermark.
```

## 3. Variantă pe o singură idee

Când ai ales un simbol din planșă, îl duci mai departe cu promptul ăsta.
Înlocuiește rândul dintre paranteze cu ideea aleasă.

```
A single logo mark for a premium French-tutoring brand, presented centred on a
warm cream background (#f8f3eb) with generous empty space around it.

THE MARK: [scrie aici ideea aleasă, de exemplu: a lowercase letter o wearing a
French acute accent, where the accent is a small tapered stroke tilted to the
right]

Drawn in one flat colour, vivid indigo blue (#4865ff), with confident even
stroke weight and geometric precision. Perfectly balanced, symmetrical where it
helps, optically corrected where symmetry would look wrong.

Beneath the mark, show the same mark three more times in a row, small: once in
near-black violet (#080331), once in white inside an indigo circle, once
reduced to 16 pixels to prove it still reads.

STYLE: contemporary editorial branding, Pentagram-grade minimalism, perfectly
flat vector look, crisp edges.

DO NOT INCLUDE: no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no texture, no mockups, no
lettering, no brand name, no watermark.
```

## 5. Planșă cu semne făcute doar din cercuri

Se potrivește cel mai bine cu site-ul, care e construit din cercuri colorate.

```
Logo exploration sheet for a warm premium education brand whose entire visual
identity is built from coloured circles on a cream canvas.

LAYOUT: flat 2D presentation board on a warm cream background (#f8f3eb). A
3 x 3 grid of nine separate logo marks, each isolated and centred in its own
generous cell of empty space. No frames, no captions, no numbers, no text.

THE NINE MARKS, all built only from circles, arcs and one small accent stroke:
1. a solid circle with a small tilted accent stroke floating just above it
2. a ring with a single gap at the top where an accent stroke sits
3. three circles of decreasing size arranged in a gentle rising diagonal
4. two circles side by side, one solid and one outlined, slightly overlapping
5. a circle made of two facing crescent halves in two different colours
6. a ring with a smaller solid circle centred inside it
7. a circle quartered into four separate arcs, each arc a different colour
8. a large ring with a tiny solid circle sitting on its upper right edge
9. a solid circle with a crescent bite taken out of its lower left

COLOUR: use only this brand palette, one or two flat colours per mark: vivid
```

## 6. Planșă cu numele, șase variante

Cea care a ieșit cel mai bine. Ține instrucțiunea de ortografie exact așa,
altfel modelul scrie greșit.

```
Typography logo lockup sheet on a warm cream background (#f8f3eb). Six wordmark
variations arranged in a calm vertical column with generous spacing, each
centred horizontally. Nothing else on the board.

THE BRAND NAME, spelled exactly: Dor de Franceza. Always set in a refined
editorial serif at regular weight, elegant and high-contrast, in the spirit of
Source Serif or Freight Display. The letter o in Dor always carries a small
French acute accent in warm coral (#ff6d39), which is the brand's signature
detail. Main text colour near-black violet (#080331).

THE SIX VARIATIONS, top to bottom:
1. Dor de Franceza on one line, the word de in italic, everything else upright
2. the same one line, but de is smaller and set in a grey sans-serif
3. Dor de Franceza stacked on two lines, Dor above and de Franceza below,
   left-aligned to each other
4. DOR DE FRANCEZA in small elegant capitals with wide letter-spacing
5. a small indigo circular badge holding a white accented letter o, placed to
   the left of the one-line wordmark
6. the one-line wordmark with a short line of small grey sans-serif capitals
   beneath it reading LECTII DE FRANCEZA ONLINE

STYLE: flat vector typography specimen, precise letterforms, correct spelling,
generous letter-spacing, no distortion, editorial, calm, expensive.

DO NOT INCLUDE: no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no texture, no mockups, no
extra words, no Eiffel Tower, no flags, no watermark.
```

## Ideile, pe scurt, și de ce

**1. Litera „o" cu accent francez.** Cuvântul românesc „dor" îmbrăcat într-un
accent franțuzesc. Două limbi într-un semn, fără să explici nimic nimănui. Se
citește și la 16 pixeli. Recomandarea mea.

**2. Ghilimelele franceze « ».** Semnul cel mai franțuzesc care există și, în
același timp, semnul că cineva vorbește. Exact ce vinde Dorina: conversație,
nu gramatică.

**3. Ușa cu arc parizian care e și litera D.** Se leagă de motivul real pentru
care oamenii vin: franceza le deschide o ușă, spre un job sau spre altă țară.

**4. Rândunica dintr-o linie.** Pasărea dorului la noi, hirondelle la ei. Caldă,
dar riscă să semene cu multe alte logouri.

**5. Monogramă D și F.** Cea mai sigură și cea mai rece. O țin pe listă ca
măsură de comparație.

**Ce am evitat dinadins:** turnul Eiffel, croasantul, bereta, tricolorul. Sunt
clișee care ieftinesc un brand care cere 211 lei pe lecție.

**Despre auriu.** Nu intră în paleta site-ului, care e crem cu indigo. Auriul
ar trage brandul spre „boutique de lux" și departe de „profesoara caldă care
îți răspunde pe WhatsApp". Dacă totuși îl vrei, chihlimbarul `#d9a441` e cel
mai apropiat de crem, dar l-aș folosi doar ca accent mic, niciodată pentru
simbolul întreg.
